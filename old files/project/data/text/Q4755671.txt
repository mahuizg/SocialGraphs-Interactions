Andreas Sebastian Weigend (born 1961) is a German-American computer scientist and author of the book Data for the People (Basic Books, 2017). He was a member of Germany's Digital Council under Merkel, Digitalrat.

Weigend studied physics and philosophy at the University of Bonn and received a PhD in physics from Stanford University in 1991. He was the Chief Scientist at Amazon.com.

 Early life and education 
Weigend studied electrical engineering before pursuing studies in physics and philosophy at Karlsruhe, Cambridge and Bonn. In 1983, he was a summer student at CERN. He later received a doctorate in physics from Stanford University.

 Academic and industry career 
Weigend worked at Amazon from 2002 to 2004 as its chief scientist. During his time there, he was involved in the company's data strategy and research into customer behaviour.

 Public service in Germany 
In August 2018, Weigend was appointed to the German government's Digital Council, an advisory body established under Chancellor Angela Merkel. He left the council in March 2019.
